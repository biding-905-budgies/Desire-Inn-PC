## 🕹️ About

Desire Inn is an adult time-management and hotel-simulation game developed by BarHat Games and published by Passion Panda Publishing. You run a growing network of pleasure-focused hotels, serving guests, coordinating staff, upgrading rooms, and unlocking new destinations such as Italy, France, and Japan. Build a team of characters with unique bonuses, improve hotel efficiency, and develop relationships through story scenes and private encounters. The game combines fast-paced service challenges, resource management, character collection, dating-sim elements, and 2D adult storytelling.

Desire Inn is for adults who enjoy casual management games with fast service challenges, character collection, dating-sim progression, and explicit 2D storytelling.

## ✨ Features

- 🌍 **Global Destinations** — Unlock new locations, hotels, employees, and story chapters around the world.
- 🎰 **Character Recruitment** — Use the game's character-drawing system to expand your available team.
- 🏆 **Achievement Support** — Track progress through a collection of in-game achievements.
- 🏨 **Hotel Management** — Operate expanding hotels by serving guests, assigning staff, and improving rooms.
- 🔞 **Adult Content** — Features mature themes, nudity, sexual content, and characters who are all over 18.
- 💬 **Relationship Stories** — Get to know hotel employees and discover personal scenes beyond the daily shift.
- 💰 **Resource Progression** — Earn money, upgrade rooms, unlock areas, and grow each hotel.
- ⏱️ **Time-Management Levels** — Handle requests quickly and keep every shift running smoothly.
- ✨ **Character Collection** — Recruit a cast of characters and build teams around their unique bonuses.
